# Claude Design hand-off, CV for LumApps

Prepared 2026-10-25. Source content: `cv-v09.md`, checked line by line against `../factbase.md`. The design pass changes layout and typography only. Wording and figures stay as written.

## What to produce

A two-page CV in LumApps' visual register, exported as PDF. English. Less is more: generous white space, one accent colour, no icons, no skill bars, no photo unless the owner adds one.

## Hierarchy to keep

1. Name, then the one-line headline "Senior programme manager, platform rollouts to adoption", then the contact line.
2. Profile paragraph, four sentences, set slightly larger than body text.
3. Experience, newest first. Each employer is a heading with place and dates. Inside SQLI and CGI Montreal, the bold sub-headings are roles or accounts and carry their own dates. Bullets are full sentences and must not be cut.
4. Education and certifications as a short list at the end.

Page break preference: SQLI and Accenture on page one, CGI onward on page two. If the profile and SQLI block need the room, Accenture may move to page two.

## LumApps brand cues, verified from public pages on 2026-10-25

- Tagline since April 2025: "The Bright Way to Work". The brand describes its look as "clean design with impactful colors and visuals" and "intuitive", built to avoid "information overload".
- Homepage headline register: short imperative phrases, "where work comes together", "turn communication into action". Confident, concrete, no superlatives in the CV itself.
- Illustrations on lumapps.com are colour coded by audience (a pink cube, green for IT, purple for HR), which suggests a bright multi-hue palette on neutral backgrounds.
- Not verified: hex values, typeface names, logo construction. The press release and the homepage text carry none. Pull the palette and type from lumapps.com directly during the design pass (computed styles or a screenshot), and prefer one accent drawn from their primary brand colour over several.

## Rules for the pass

- Do not reword, shorten or reorder bullets. If a bullet does not fit, adjust spacing or type size, not the text.
- Keep straight quotes, no em or en dashes, no semicolons, no emoji.
- Headings keep sentence case.
- The luxury client stays unnamed. The programme names stay out.
- Return the PDF plus the editable source so later versions can be re-rendered.
