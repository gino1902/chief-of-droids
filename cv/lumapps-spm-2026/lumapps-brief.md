# LumApps brief

Researched on 2026-10-25 by a research subagent from public sources. The URLs at the end are the pages it opened. I did not re-open them. What it could not confirm sits under "Unverified".

## Company

LumApps was founded in Lyon in 2012 by Sébastien Ricard, Élie Mélois, Olivier Chanoux and Lionel Grivel. The registered seat is in Tassin-la-Demi-Lune, with Austin, Texas presented as the US headquarters. The company page lists nine offices across France, the UK, Germany, Spain, Japan and the US. Job postings add Zurich and Krakow. There is no Geneva office. Headcount reads over 600 on the company page and about 400 on the careers site. The company claims over 2,200 customers and 7 million daily users.

Bridgepoint became majority owner in May 2024, in a deal reported at 650 million dollars, buying out Goldman Sachs Growth Equity, Eurazeo, Bpifrance and IRIS. Founders and management kept stakes. In July 2025 Beekeeper, a Zurich vendor of frontline worker apps, joined LumApps. The combined company passed a billion-dollar valuation with about 600 staff and about 150 million dollars of recurring revenue. In April 2026 LumApps agreed to acquire Comeen (room booking, signage, visitor management).

Sébastien Ricard stays CEO until 1 December 2026. Sébastien Cano, formerly of Thales and Gemalto and based in Austin, then takes over and Ricard becomes executive chairman (announced 8 September 2026). Other markers: the "Bright Way to Work" rebrand in April 2025, the LumApps AI launch on 23 June 2026, and a fourth consecutive Leader position in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Intranet Packaged Solutions (5 October 2026).

## Product

LumApps calls itself "the AI Employee Hub where work comes together". It has three lines. The core hub covers communications, communities, knowledge and enterprise search. Frontline Workers is the former Beekeeper, mobile first, for retail, manufacturing and healthcare. Workplace Experience is the former Comeen. LumApps AI bundles an Agent Hub, an Agent Studio and Workflows and actions, and a forward-deployment engineering team builds bespoke agents. Google customers run on Vertex AI, Microsoft customers on Azure GPT. Integrations are native for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 and cover Teams, SharePoint, Slack, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, ServiceNow and UKG, with a claim of over 100 connectors. The SaaS runs replicated on Google Cloud and Azure, with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II.

## How they deliver

A mixed model. An in-house Professional Services EMEA team of about 15 people, under a Head of Project Management, runs projects from requirements to deployment and hands over to Customer Success, where a customer success manager drives adoption. Certified partners (SADA, Onix, Wedocom) deploy under "the LumApps project methodology", which is not published. Two retail case studies show the rollout shape. Galeries Lafayette replaced a 2012 intranet from 2019 with central communication governance, one relay per brand and one page per store, for a workforce that is 75 percent deskless, reaching 7,800 monthly users. ETAM connected over 1,000 stores through in-store iPads on Microsoft 365, implemented in six months.

## Culture

The four stated values are "Be bold, do amazing things", "One team, same mission", "Say what you mean, do what you say" and "Always be evolving". The mission is "to make people feel that they belong and have a purpose". Employees are called Lumies. Hybrid work is two office days and three remote in Paris and Zurich. The careers site is in English. The Paris project manager posting is in French and requires both languages. The Zurich role adds German.

## Role profile, reconstructed

The only open posting close to the role is "Chef·fe de projet Senior, Workplace Experience (EMEA)", in Professional Services EMEA, Paris hybrid. The holder owns schedule, scope, budget, implementation plan and risk, coordinates internal and external resources, gives the client a regular status, reports to governance bodies, hands over to Customer Success and improves the company methodology. It asks for four or more years of project management, client-facing software or web platform deployment, executive-level communication and fluent French and English. SaaS deployment experience and tools such as Teams, Klaxoon, Miro or Notion are nice to have. Two closed Beekeeper postings (an EMEA project manager in Paris or Germany, an onboarding customer success manager in Zurich) carry the same duties and add German, large international customers, change management and workshop facilitation. No posting matches a core-hub rollout for a Swiss luxury group, which fits the channel: the role is not public.

## Luxury and Switzerland

Luxury retail references are Galeries Lafayette and ETAM. LVMH is named as a customer in the October 2026 Gartner release, without a case study. Switzerland is covered by the Zurich office inherited from Beekeeper. No public link between LumApps and Richemont appears on any page opened.

## What this means for the CV

- The posting's verbs are the CV's verbs: own scope, schedule, budget and risk, report to governance, hand over to run, improve the methodology. The fact base has a direct match for each, with the 15 M USD fixed-price Oracle Cloud ERP rollout over 40 sites, the CAE operating model taken to adoption, the playbook written to repeat it, and the practice management framework at Airbus.
- The floor is four years of project management and the owner has twenty. The CV must read as a delivery leader who still runs projects, not as a group executive. The rank signals (240 M EUR, 2,500 people, 40 M USD P&L) move down or out, and the delivered scopes (sites, stores, FTE, budgets) stay.
- "Say what you mean, do what you say" rewards the fact base's own discipline: no unevidenced metric, mechanism and scope stated as fact.
- Microsoft 365 and Workday are the two integration names to print. Richemont runs Exchange and has just implemented Workday (see the Richemont brief), and the owner oversaw Workday interfaces at CAE.
- Frontline Workers makes the retail record relevant: 4,500 supermarkets and point-of-sale scope at Carrefour and Auchan, and boutique-facing delivery on the maison programme.
- LumApps AI gives the Oxygen work a place as a one-line differentiator, not a headline.
- The CEO changes on 1 December 2026. Which CEO the go-between knows matters for timing. Cano's Thales past is a conversation hook, since the owner advised Thales at Accenture, not a CV line.
