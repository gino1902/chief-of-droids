## First impression

A services executive who has twice turned a hostile account into a renewal, now asking to be a project manager again. The numbers I believe. What the CV avoids is why a man who reports to a CEO stays two years under my head of project management.

## Verdict

Interview. CAE and Airbus are dated turnarounds with figures I can check, which my last two hires lacked, and retention is a thirty-minute conversation, not a CV fix.

## The five changes I would make

1. Line 11: lead with the maison and the 40-site rollout, push the AI engine to the third sentence, and name the level you are applying for.
2. Line 19: date the end or state the handover, because leaving an 18-month programme four months in is the pattern that cost me a hire.
3. Lines 15 to 33: a director title over three overlapping sub-roles, and "two years transforming internally" contradicts eighteen months on a client programme, so date each assignment separately.
4. Line 32: "support to account management on sales strategy" is a job description, so give me the sponsor, the escalation record and what SLA and margin did.
5. Lines 11 and 24: back "hands on" with one dated, checkable artefact or drop it, since here it means configuration, workshops and status decks.

## Lines I would cut

- "with agents fitted to how each team already works rather than a new operating model"
- "four to six prototypes to be selected in November 2026 together with seven senior leaders"
- "each with a dedicated lead from a Bain, Accenture, CGI consulting or banking background"
- "2008 award for project management in an international context."
- "Jazz guitar, CIM Paris"

## Three questions I would ask

1. Your programme started in June, first increment in December. Why are we talking in October, and who finishes it?
2. The worst month on the maison account: who escalated, to whom, and what did you do the week after?
3. Show me the last thing you built yourself, then your number, because a VP's salary buys me two project managers.

## What is missing

- Intranet or employee experience work, beyond "an employee portal" on line 23 and "SharePoint platform" on line 50.
- A day at a product vendor. Every employer is a services firm or his own start-up.
- Federated governance named as such, which 23 brands demand and six European agencies or 4,500 stores could prove.
- An adoption figure after go-live, which the headline promises.
- Notice period, and the level or package he is targeting.
