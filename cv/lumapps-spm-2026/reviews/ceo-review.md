# CEO review, Gilles Mourgues CV

## 1. First impression

A CGI vice-president and a CEO-reporting director applying under a senior programme manager headline, with two dated account turnarounds that carry numbers. The CV never says why he wants a smaller seat, and that silence is what I notice first.

## 2. Verdict

Interview. He has twice taken a hostile or crisis account to a renewal with numbers (CAE 2019, Airbus 2016), the one thing my last delivery lead could not do.

## 3. The five changes I would make

1. Line 11: say why a director reporting to a CEO wants a project role under a head of project management, because unexplained it reads as a demotion and I have been burnt by that once.
2. Lines 19 and 23: "to 2027" and "selected in November 2026" are future dates written as facts, so state what has shipped and mark the rest as due.
3. Lines 35 to 36: replace the nouns with who you faced at the maison, how often it escalated and what the client said at handover, because "revenue held" after a nearshore move reads as a cost cut, not an account growing.
4. Line 11: replace "Claude Code engineering, proof-of-concept design thinking" with one thing in production today, dated, with a user count.
5. Line 51: add the end-user number after the 36-site rollout, because the headline promises adoption and no line gives one.

## 4. Lines I would cut

- "The stance is invite over impose"
- "Designed as the enabler for cross-selling and capacity planning across nine countries."
- "a new operating model with clearer decision rights and talent practices fitted to client and employee expectations"
- "Earlier: a pilot training mobile prototype in React, an operational data platform on Azure."
- "Building Sustainable Relationships at C Level, CGI, 2018"

## 5. Three questions I would ask

1. Your first increment is due in December and we are talking in October. Why leave before it ships, and what keeps you here in month nine when the seat feels small?
2. At the maison, who on the client side called you when something broke, how often, and may I phone that person?
3. Show me the last pipeline you wrote, when it last ran in production and who depends on it.

## 6. What is missing

- Any intranet, communications or employee experience platform. Every rollout here is ERP, SAP or application services.
- An end-user adoption figure, anywhere.
- A client reference I can call. The maison is anonymised, CAE and Airbus are named but nobody is.
- Why now, availability, mobility to Geneva and 23 brands, and a signal on pay. Three director-level titles say he costs more than my band, and nothing says he accepts that.
- Microsoft 365, Teams or SharePoint by name. Azure is there, the client's workplace is not.

## 7. The top block, redrafted

Gilles Mourgues

Senior programme manager. Platform rollouts taken to adoption.

Twenty years of client-facing delivery in France, Canada and Morocco: a 150-person programme for a luxury maison, a 40-site cloud ERP rollout, offshore projects throughout.
