# Recruiter review, cv-v08

## 1. First impression

A CEO-reporting director and AI architect, four months into an eighteen-month programme, is asking for a senior project manager seat, and the first block he shows me is the AI programme, not the luxury maison or the 40-site SaaS rollout. The fit is real underneath, Lyon, Geneva on site, luxury, SaaS rollout to adoption, but I had to dig for it.

## 2. Verdict

Maybe. The page does not say why a director four months into a programme he designed wants a seat a grade and a pay band below, so the hiring manager will read salary, stopgap, or both.

## 3. The five changes I would make

1. Line 11: lead with the luxury maison and the SaaS rollout, shrink the AI engine to one clause, and fix "After two years transforming internally", which lines 30 to 33 contradict.
2. Lines 19 to 24: two bullets only, team of ten, ExCo steering, December increment, adoption approach, because today the block describes a data architect.
3. Line 7: "English professional" becomes "English fluent, six years in Montreal", the posting says fluent.
4. Lines 32 and 33: name who you handed over to, give "up 10 percent" a unit, and use the posting's words, scope, schedule, budget, risk, client status, steering committee.
5. Line 48: split ownership (scope, schedule, budget, risk, pilot, rollout, adoption, change management) from architecture and add "SaaS deployment", this is the line the ATS and the hiring manager both score.

## 4. Lines I would cut

- "reporting to the CEO" and "and one-to-ones with the CEO"
- "Operations orchestration. One governed source of trusted data on Azure Databricks ..." (whole bullet)
- "Delivery accelerators. The group's delivery assets turned into AI accelerators ..." (whole bullet)
- "each with a dedicated lead from a Bain, Accenture, CGI consulting or banking background"
- "where I built the front end and the back end" and "LAMP stack, eXtreme Programming."

## 5. Three questions a hiring manager will ask

1. Why leave a director role four months into a programme you designed, what do you expect to be paid, and will you still be here in two years?
2. Which luxury maison, is it the group we are rolling out for, and did you run the plan and the client status yourself or through project managers under you?
3. Have you deployed an intranet or employee-facing platform, and when did you last write a project plan and run a weekly client status with your own hands?

## 6. What is missing

Role profile words absent from the page: deployment, implementation plan, status, stakeholder, customer success, handover as a noun, methodology, change management as something you did rather than an offering, workshop facilitation, fluent, international customers. Nice-to-haves absent: Teams, Miro, Klaxoon, Notion, German. Facts missing or buried: the SharePoint platform at CAE is your only intranet-adjacent line and sits inside a Scrumban sentence, the 2002 to 2006 SaaS product architect years prove you know vendor-side delivery and the profile ignores them, the CGI to Accenture overlap in May 2022 needs a date fix, and a French version for the Lyon HR screen.

## 7. Top block, redrafted

Senior project manager, SaaS platform rollouts taken to adoption

Twenty years of client-facing delivery in France, Canada and Morocco, offshore teams throughout: a 150-person programme for a luxury maison, a 40-site cloud ERP SaaS rollout taken to adoption. Two years into my own group's transformation, where I build the AI engine for 2,500 people across nine countries, I want to deliver for clients again, on a vendor's product, hands on. Hands on today means data and AI architecture, Databricks and Claude Code engineering, proof-of-concept design thinking workshops. Self-taught programmer.
