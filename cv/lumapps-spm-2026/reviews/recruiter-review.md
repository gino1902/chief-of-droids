# Recruiter review

## First impression

A CGI vice-president and Accenture associate director, now architecting an AI platform for his own employer, asking for a project manager's seat: my gut says he will cost too much and leave within a year. The rollout proof is on the page, the 40-site cloud ERP and the luxury maison programme, but it sits under a headline and a profile that sell something else.

## Verdict

Maybe. The record fits the assignment, but the page never says why a director reporting to a CEO wants this job, so the hiring manager will assume the worst on pay and tenure.

## The five changes I would make

1. Line 11: open the profile with the client rollouts and one sentence on why a senior project manager role now, with the AI engine last.
2. Line 5: write "senior project manager", the title the hiring manager and the ATS search for, since "programme manager" reads one level up.
3. Lines 19 to 26: cut the AI engine block to two bullets and remove every date after today (2027, November 2026, December 2026), because a future outcome reads as a plan.
4. Lines 33 to 36: make the luxury maison programme the longest block, saying what was delivered, for which functions and countries, on which platforms.
5. Line 87: move the languages to line 7 beside the contact details, with a German level if any, since fluent French and English is the posting's one hard requirement.

## Lines I would cut

- "Model the data, build the pipelines and the Gold layer, set up the platform engineering and architect the solution myself. Steer the infrastructure (network, storage, compute, Terraform) and the use cases." (line 26)
- "Earlier: a pilot training mobile prototype in React, an operational data platform on Azure." (line 54)
- "Building Sustainable Relationships at C Level, CGI, 2018" (line 86)
- Line 80, the start-up paragraph: keep one line, the two companies and the dates.

## Three questions a hiring manager will ask

1. Why does a director reporting to a CEO want a senior project manager role, and at what pay?
2. When did he last run a client project himself, status report and plan included, given the last client-facing role ended in December 2025?
3. Has he ever deployed an intranet, an employee-facing SaaS or a digital workplace, rather than ERP and application outsourcing?

## What is missing

The posting's vocabulary: scope, schedule, risk, implementation plan, status, governance bodies, handover to Customer Success, methodology, workshop facilitation, stakeholder, and change management (named once, as an offering). Also absent: SaaS deployment as a phrase, intranet, employee experience, digital workplace, German, Teams, Miro, Klaxoon, Notion, and on-site availability in Switzerland. The only link to this vendor's product, "an employee portal" on line 23, is buried. Line 36, "team up 10 percent and revenue held" after a nearshore move, does not say what the client gained.

## The top block, redrafted

Senior project manager, platform rollouts taken to adoption

Twenty years of client-facing delivery in France, Canada and Morocco: a 40-site cloud ERP rollout, a 150-person programme for a luxury maison, offshore projects throughout. Rollouts reach adoption when teams are brought on with what they already use, so that is where I start and the change effort stays small. Today I build the AI engine of a company-wide transformation, 2,500 people across nine countries. Self-taught programmer, still hands on: data and AI architecture, Databricks engineering, Claude Code engineering, proof-of-concept design thinking.
