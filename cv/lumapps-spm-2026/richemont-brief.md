# Richemont brief

Researched on 2026-10-25 by a research subagent from public sources. The URLs at the end are the pages it opened. I did not re-open them. What it could not confirm sits under "Unverified". The client is never named on the CV. This file is for the owner's preparation only.

## Group

Richemont's registered office is in Bellevue, Geneva, and the central teams are "mostly based at our Headquarters in Geneva". At 31 March 2026 the group employed 40,465 people of 133 nationalities in 40 countries, 56 percent of them in Europe. FY26 sales were 22.4 billion euros, operating profit 4.5 billion euros, net cash 8.5 billion euros.

There are 23 maisons in three business areas. Jewellery: Buccellati, Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Vhernier (16.5 billion euros of sales). Specialist Watchmakers: A. Lange & Söhne, IWC, Jaeger-LeCoultre, Panerai, Piaget, Roger Dubuis, Vacheron Constantin (3.1 billion euros). Other: Alaïa, Chloé, Delvaux, dunhill, G/FORE, Gianvito Rossi, Montblanc, Peter Millar, Purdey, Serapian, TimeVallée, Watchfinder (2.7 billion euros, loss making).

In the group's own words, "each of the Maisons in the Group enjoys a high degree of autonomy, with its own management group under a Chief Executive Officer". Central and regional functions provide "distribution, finance, legal, IT and administration services" and "constitute a Services Framework to support the Maisons". Regional offices sit in Geneva, New York, Hong Kong, Tokyo and Dubai.

Nicolas Bos has been Group CEO since June 2024, reporting to chairman Johann Rupert. The Senior Executive Committee includes Burkhart Grund (CFO), Karlheinz Baumann (Operations), Patricia Gandji (CEO of Regions), Renaud Litré (Chief Platforms Officer, on the committee since January 2026), Marie-Aude Stocker (Chief People Officer since January 2025) and the CEOs of Cartier and Van Cleef & Arpels. The Swiss footprint covers the Bellevue head office, the Meyrin campus, Villars-sur-Glâne, ValFleurier and manufactures in Plan-les-Ouates, La Côte-aux-Fées, Le Sentier, Le Locle, Neuchâtel and Schaffhausen. "Most corporate costs are incurred in Switzerland."

## People and culture

The FY26 reports describe "commitment to excellence and respect for artistry", "a sense of belonging", a leadership culture of "responsibility and accountability" and "a mature culture relying on trust and mutual respect". Group HR says it works "whilst respecting each Maison's identity and singularity". Employer brand lines are "Let's craft the future together" and "We treasure our colleagues". Average seniority is 8 years and training averages 16 hours a year.

FY26 HR priorities: Workday went live as the group HRIS, senior leaders are now assessed on how they meet their objectives, upskilling includes technology, and a craftsmanship and apprenticeship function was created. Group-wide programmes include digital onboarding (since FY24), the WeCare assistance and Speak Up platforms, online Sustainability and Creative academies, the EMERGE leadership programme in Europe and a "Swiss HR Call". No statement on working languages was found. Meyrin postings require fluent English, with French an advantage.

## Digital workplace and IT

Technology sits in Group Platforms under Renaud Litré, "enabling the Maisons, functions and regions" through shared "technological foundations, solutions and infrastructure". Kim Hartlev has been Group CIO since 2019. Teams are mainly in Geneva and Lisbon, where R:TECH, a technology centre opened in 2025, had hired 100 people by mid-2025 with a target of 400 by 2028, covering platforms, business applications, data and AI. FY26 delivered a new e-commerce platform, a unified Data and AI platform and a Digital Product team across Retail, Client, Marketing, Operations, Finance and HR, built on "a federated architectural vision designed to promote greater autonomy, coherence, scalability and efficiency".

An intranet exists. The FY26 non-financial report cites "the Group's intranet" for policies and compliance training (36,375 completions). In 2019 the group reported one group intranet, 11 maison intranet sites and an internal social network with 330 active users. Richemont joined the Digital Workplace Group in 2016 and showed its global intranet there in 2018. No vendor is named. Collaboration runs on Microsoft. A Meyrin unified communications team runs 50,000 mailboxes on Exchange, and workplace postings cite Office, Outlook, Active Directory and JAMF. No Google Workspace reference was found. An AI council and an internal employee agent ("Agent 42") were described in May 2025 in one Portuguese source. No public link between Richemont and LumApps was found.

## Recent news, 2025 to 2026

Press reported in March 2025 that the Specialist Watchmakers division was dissolved to give the maisons more autonomy, though FY26 reporting still uses the business area. YNAP was sold to Mytheresa in April 2025. Baume & Mercier was sold to Damiani, completed in July 2026, with Richemont supplying operational services for at least 12 months. FY26 results in May 2026 carried 99 million euros of impairments. Anton Rupert became co-deputy chairman in September 2026. Interim results are due on 13 November 2026. No group-wide cost programme was announced.

## What this means for the CV

- The project is a federated replacement, not a greenfield build: one group platform, maison relays that respect "each Maison's identity and singularity", and a legacy of one group intranet plus 11 maison sites. The CV's closest matches are the SQLI operating model built across nine countries against local-entity reflexes, the Montreal practice run horizontal to autonomous sectors, and the 40-site ERP rollout with a pilot wave.
- Microsoft and Workday are the stack. Print both names.
- Geneva plus Lisbon mirrors the owner's onshore and nearshore delivery record (Morocco, India and Portugal in the Airbus programme). One line.
- Belonging is the word LumApps' mission and Richemont's culture statement share. The summary can carry it once. The owner's inclusion record (consultants with disabilities integrated at Airbus) matches "respect" and "trust" in one line, not as a headline.
- Group Platforms, People and the CIO are the likely sponsors. Executive governance proof (reporting to a CEO, rebuilding a CIO relationship at CAE) earns its place.
- Richemont's Data and AI platform and employee agent give the Oxygen line a client-side echo.
- Central teams are in Geneva and Meyrin postings want fluent English. The CV header should say Lyon and on-site availability in Geneva once the owner settles mobility.
