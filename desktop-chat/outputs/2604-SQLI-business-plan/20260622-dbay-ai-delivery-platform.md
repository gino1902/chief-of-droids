# Agentic Delivery Platform

<!-- slide 1 -->
## Vision

Agentic Delivery Platform build on 3 pillars

Reusable Assets
Agentic Delivery Platform to augment our services by using proven and reusable assets which **differentiates us** and **increase our service value**.

Adaptation to Context
A place where the end to end agentic delivery toolchain can be tailored to each of our clients, whith guardrails to garantee quality and predictability of our delivery.

Governed by a community
best proven assets selected from real projects and standardize for consistent reuse across across SQLI. Complemented by best AI market tools.


## Assets Collection

###
A SQLI Community governance is set up to monitor and surface the assets used for the activities SQLI delivers, from Project to Staff Augmentation services*, including sales offerings.

###
The community reviews and selects the best proven assets across SQLI projects (eg Bayard, Ceva).

###
The assets collection covers the end-to-end delivery lifecycle so end-to-end activities and deliverables are covered.

(*) Knowledge assets, deliverable assets, fitting to mission context and client industry. 

## Reusability process
- Selected assets are run through a quality assurance process, to ensure the best outcome with good predictability.

- Asset documentation is polished so it can be well understood and adapted by teams to real-life work.

### Adaptation to Context
At project start (or mission start for staff augmentation), the assets covering the whole SDLC and best fitting to client context are selected by the team.

The platform ensures the consistency of the selection and points out gaps and necessary adjustments.

A draft of the Project documentation can be generated, tailored to the assets selection and the client context.

For services relying on activity automation and repeatability (eg Managed Service, QA), **pre-built orchestrators** are available. This ensures consistency and quality across SQLI, enabling improvement at scale.
