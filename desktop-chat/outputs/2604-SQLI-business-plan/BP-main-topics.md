|  # | Topic to cover                                              | Why it belongs in the 2028 plan                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -: | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  1 | **2028 strategic ambition and investment thesis**           | Define what the company should become despite revenue contraction: resilient IT services platform, AI-enabled delivery partner, nearshore specialist, sector-focused provider, or managed-services operator. |
|  2 | **Value-creation thesis under revenue shrinkage**           | Clarify the few levers that will create value: margin resilience, recurring revenue, delivery productivity, client retention, cross-sell, vendor consolidation capture, and risk reduction.                  |
|  3 | **Target client portfolio and segmentation**                | Separate midmarket, large accounts, industry leaders, public sector if relevant, and define which segments deserve investment versus protection.                                                             |
|  4 | **Core offer portfolio for 2028**                           | Define which services should be scaled, defended, transformed, or exited: application services, cloud, data, cybersecurity, AI, ERP, managed services, product engineering, and run/operate services.        |
|  5 | **AI-enabled service model**                                | Explain how AI changes delivery, productivity, coding, testing, support, knowledge management, and quality assurance. This is a central value lever, not a side topic.                                       |
|  6 | **Recurring and managed-services growth model**             | Prioritize predictable revenue streams: application maintenance, platform operations, security operations, data operations, AI operations, and business-process-enabled IT services.                         |
|  7 | **Delivery model and France–North Africa operating model**  | Convert the geographic footprint into a value argument: client intimacy in France, scalable nearshore delivery in North Africa, bilingual/French-speaking delivery, cost competitiveness, and talent depth.  |
|  8 | **Industrialized delivery capabilities**                    | Cover methods, reusable assets, accelerators, delivery governance, QA, DevSecOps, automation, and standardization that improve margin and reduce project risk.                                               |
|  9 | **Strategic account management and sales engine**           | Define how the company protects existing revenue, expands accounts, captures vendor consolidation, and sells higher-value work during client budget pressure.                                                |
| 10 | **Sector and domain specialization**                        | Identify where the company must become sharper by industry or business domain. Generic IT services will be harder to defend under price pressure.                                                            |
| 11 | **Talent and capability model**                             | Cover reskilling, pyramid structure, senior expert density, AI-augmented roles, nearshore career paths, retention, and productivity per employee.                                                            |
| 12 | **Technology platforms, tools, and automation stack**       | Include only tools that improve commercial conversion, delivery quality, delivery speed, knowledge reuse, margin, or control. Avoid a tool inventory.                                                        |
| 13 | **Partner ecosystem and alliances**                         | Define priority partnerships with cloud providers, software vendors, AI platforms, cybersecurity vendors, ERP providers, and niche specialists.                                                              |
| 14 | **Client value proposition and differentiation**            | State why customers should choose this company in 2028: lower risk, better French/nearshore model, faster delivery, AI-enabled productivity, sector expertise, or superior run capability.                   |
| 15 | **Operating governance and management system**              | Cover decision rights, performance cadence, portfolio governance, delivery assurance, pricing discipline, and investment arbitration.                                                                        |
| 16 | **Risk, compliance, sovereignty, and security positioning** | Especially relevant for EU-based clients: data sovereignty, cybersecurity, AI governance, regulatory alignment, and delivery continuity.                                                                     |
| 17 | **M&A and partnership options**                             | Since this is M&A-relevant, cover potential acquisition themes: AI delivery boutiques, cybersecurity, cloud operations, ERP capability, sector specialists, or North Africa delivery expansion.              |
| 18 | **Execution roadmap and transformation agenda**             | Translate the plan into major transformation waves: portfolio cleanup, AI delivery industrialization, sales refocus, talent reskilling, operating model redesign, and tooling rollout.                       |
