# Agentic Foreseen Disruptions

## 1. Customer & Buying Journey Disruption

- Customer interactions with brands will increasingly be managed by agents.
- Customers will increasingly choose products with LLM support.
- AI end user point will copilot the sales act based on a ad-hoc requests ("I want to watch a movie with steve mcqueen") and on consumer private context (subscriptions to streaming platform). 
- AI end user point can autopilot the sales act based on bulk requests (pictures of supermarket shelves) and on consumer private context (consumer profile, history).
- User existing digital endpoints will be complemented/coupled by/to AI assistants
- AI assistant will be user configurable, industry specialised or generalist.
- AI assistants will become consolidation points for product comparison and buying.

## 2. Agentic Architecture & Navigation Layer

- Websites make business workflows navigable only for humans.
- MCP servers make business workflows navigable for agents.
- MCP servers create a new navigation layer to design, build, and supervise.
- MCP navigation layer must be coupled with existing web interfaces, APIs, data contracts, workflows, and security models.

## 3. Trust, Security & Control Model

- In the AI age, data security, rather than technology alone, will drive use case acceptability.
- AI use cases security compliance will be driven by human points of control design.
- Clients will need to understand how to trust an agent.
- Clients will need to understand what is done by an agent versus a human.
- Clients will need to understand what they are buying from an IT service provider.

## 4. Delivery & Workforce Transformation

- AI will require new jobs specialised in agent steering and supervision.
- Agent supervision will cover access security.
- Agent supervision will cover company policy compliance.
- Agent supervision will cover agent activity tracability.
- Agent steering will cover exception management.
- Agent steering will cover company quality compliance.
- Agent steering will cover hard gate management.
- Coding languages will become secondary in favour of algorithms, logic, and agent steering / supervision.
- Steering / supervising a team of agents’ work will become as important as managing personal work.
- Steering / supervising a team of agents’ work will require specific behaviour skills (inter-agentic skills).
- Companies will manage a dual workforce over the next 10 to 20 years AI natives, AI adopters.
- This new workforce will include AI-native new graduates.
- This new workforce will include seasoned workers who need to move away from coding.

## 5. Ecosystem, Sovereignty & Supply Chain Resilience

- Sovereignty will become a primary concern in the EU.
- Supply chain control will become a primary concern in the EU.
- Partnership ecosystems will primarly take into account Sovereignty and supply chain control.

## 6. Economic Model & Value Chain Shift

- LLMs will be standardised in cloud infrastructure.
- LLMs will become a commodity.
- SaaS integration will decrease fast in favour of workflow orchestration.
- Revenue distribution will shift from the workforce & software value chain to the orchestration value chain.
