# overview.md template

Fill this structure. Omit a section if the wiki has nothing for it. Keep prose tight; the value is orientation, not duplication. Every named theme, entity, and concept links to its `[[page]]`.

`overview.md` is a framework synthesis file, so it carries the version block (the page-schema rule's carve-out). It is not an entity/concept/synthesis content page and has no front-matter or `*Sources:*` lines.

```markdown
# Wiki overview

> High-level synthesis. Generated by /authoring-overview on <YYYY-MM-DD>. A snapshot, not live; regenerate to refresh.

## What this wiki covers

<Two to four sentences: the subject area, its scope, and the rough scale (how many pages, the dominant themes). Derived from the spread of pages and tags.>

## Map by theme

<One short paragraph per theme (tag or cluster). Name what the theme is about and link the pages under it. A page may appear under more than one theme.>

- **<theme>** — <one line>. [[page-a]], [[page-b]], [[page-c]]
- **<theme>** — <one line>. [[page-d]], [[page-e]]

## Key entities

<The most central entity pages, by inbound links. One line each.>

- [[entity-a]] — <one-line role>
- [[entity-b]] — <one-line role>

## Key concepts

<The most central concept pages. One line each.>

- [[concept-a]] — <one-line definition>
- [[concept-b]] — <one-line definition>

## Open threads

<Optional and light. Synthesised from open questions and thin spots across pages — what the wiki raises but does not yet settle. Link the pages where each thread lives. Omit the section if there is nothing substantive.>

- <thread> — [[page]]

| Field        | Value        |
|--------------|--------------|
| Version      | 1.0          |
| Last Updated | <YYYY-MM-DD> |
| Status       | Draft        |
```

Notes:

- The version block inside the generated `overview.md` tracks the overview document itself. Bump its `Version` on each regeneration that materially changes the content, and set `Last Updated` to the generation date.
- Use `[[slug]]` link targets that match page filenames so Obsidian and the ingest pipeline resolve them.

## Worked example — small wiki

```markdown
# Wiki overview

> High-level synthesis. Generated by /authoring-overview on 2026-05-31. A snapshot, not live; regenerate to refresh.

## What this wiki covers

The automotive and clean-energy activities of Tesla and its founder, across 7 pages. The dominant themes are the company itself, its products, and the people around it.

## Map by theme

- **auto-industry** — the company, its vehicles, and its manufacturing. [[tesla-inc]], [[cybertruck]], [[gigafactory-texas]]
- **people** — founders and executives. [[elon-musk]]

## Key entities

- [[tesla-inc]] — the electric-vehicle and energy company
- [[elon-musk]] — founder and CEO

## Key concepts

- [[battery-day]] — the company's recurring cell-technology event

## Open threads

- Whether the Dojo programme is still funded — [[dojo-supercomputer]]

| Field        | Value      |
|--------------|------------|
| Version      | 1.0        |
| Last Updated | 2026-05-31 |
| Status       | Draft      |
```

## Worked example — sparse wiki (edge case)

When no themes or centres emerge, do not invent groupings:

```markdown
# Wiki overview

> High-level synthesis. Generated by /authoring-overview on 2026-05-31. A snapshot, not live; regenerate to refresh.

## What this wiki covers

3 pages, too few and too lightly linked to group into themes. Listed flatly below.

- [[tesla-inc]] — entity
- [[elon-musk]] — entity
- [[battery-day]] — concept

| Field        | Value      |
|--------------|------------|
| Version      | 1.0        |
| Last Updated | 2026-05-31 |
| Status       | Draft      |
```

## Map vs rewrite

Correct (map: orient and link):
> - **auto-industry** — the company, its vehicles, and its manufacturing. [[tesla-inc]], [[cybertruck]]

Incorrect (rewrite: re-states page content the reader should get from the page):
> - **auto-industry** — Tesla was founded in 2003 in San Carlos, California, and released the Roadster in 2008, followed by the Model S in 2012, the Model X in 2015 […]
